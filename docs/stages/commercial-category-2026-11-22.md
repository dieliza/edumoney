# Unified Commercial category with separate business accounting

Removed the two create-income/create-expense buttons from Business transactions. Regular New transaction retains its Gasto/Ingreso controls and gains a virtual Comercial category for each direction. A green bold selected-context notice explains separation. One supported DBA/LLC is auto-selected; multiple profiles require explicit selection; no valid profile means no save. An optional description identifies the commercial transaction. Commercial recurring income is not silently scheduled through the personal scheduler.

MoneyStore.saveCommercial atomically validates the holder, category, date, amount, duplicate identity and links; persists the movement with business use; creates/links its existing business ledger record and full allocation under Actividad comercial; and keeps CPA status pending without assuming deductibility. Existing matching category/project names are reused. Retries retain one movement/entry/allocation. Personal-to-commercial reclassification preserves ID and revision history, rejects stale/deleted/linked operations and cannot move an assigned transaction to another business. No schema or migration changes. No new external APIs.

The personal snapshot flag excludes explicit business-use movements and whole-commercial business ledger links without deleting the general ledger. Home, personal history and charts request that scope. Monthly and historical graph models also exclude explicit business use. The Business ledger/export retain their independent raw rows and show Ingreso comercial/Gasto comercial with separate totals. Existing mixed-use records are not automatically rewritten or assigned a new percentage. Voice text requesting Commercial remains pending until the user chooses the appropriate business in the standard form; no business is inferred from speech.
