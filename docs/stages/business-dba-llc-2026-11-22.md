# Business simplification for DBA and LLC

Only Business UI changed. New profiles offer DBA (existing individual representation) or LLC. Existing legal names, DBA associations, merchant values, tax classifications and projects remain in the same data model and are preserved on save. Project creation and classification questions were removed from the simplified form. LLC constitution and membership fields retain their existing validation. Existing unsupported profiles retain access to their recorded transactions/tools without silent conversion or deletion.

Business opens with the profile list rather than a long automatic form. Transactions and Payroll are primary actions. Accountant, business paystubs and IVU/contractors are grouped behind Documents and tools. The planning/scenario shortcut is removed from this page; stored scenarios are not deleted. No personal UI, accounting calculations, schema, API, auth, synchronization or tax rules changed.

Payroll route now defers screen loading inside Suspense and a local retryable error boundary. PDF report/download imports are deferred until the export action. This removes their initialization from screen opening and prevents a loading/rendering error from leaving an empty page. Calculations and confirmed payroll records are unchanged. Real browser/device reproduction of the reported blank screen was unavailable; automated SSR/module tests validate the mitigations but do not establish the specific error on the user's device.

Publication baseline reconciled from live Sites version 78, source 9f4d012f5dfe19fc662fd42a72e876fdd8391154. The local full build was older; replacing it would regress newer account/recovery/server changes. scripts/patch-business-site.cjs instead transpiles and replaces only factories 794,795,913,914 in the current live asset map. Updated chunk URLs and their importers are versioned for cache invalidation. All server code outside ROUTES/CONTENT is preserved byte-for-byte; unrelated asset contents are retained except references to the renamed chunks. Reproduce by extracting that baseline worker to /tmp/edumoney-live78-worker.js from the hosting repository before running the script. Source TSX edits are tracked here for future full source reconciliation.
